Oklahoma Senate Bill 626

Oklahoma only. Senate Bill 626, effective January 1, 2026, lets a business raise an affirmative defense under the Security Breach Notification Act when it keeps reasonable safeguards and gives the notice the statute requires. Rooted Tech is not a law firm. The scan is not a certificate, and it is not immunity.

The scan is written against

  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework: Identify, Protect, and Detect.
  • CIS, alongside NIST, as named in the rules of engagement.
  • SB 626 reasonable safeguards: risk assessment, layered technical and physical defenses, employee training, and an incident response plan.
